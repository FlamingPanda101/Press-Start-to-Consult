# Art Bible: Press Start to Consult

*Cosmo the Cougar's Case Interview Strategy Guide. Image prompts for the designer, one entry per placeholder.*

## Style Block

**Palette.** Work in three inks and one shadow. Put BYU blue #002E5D on jerseys, UI frames, night skies, ink, and outlines. Put white #FFFFFF on highlights, text over navy, and the Y. Fill Cosmo's fur, parchment, stone, coins, and warm areas with tan #F5E6C8. Use a dark navy shadow tone (#001A38) for depth, drop shadows, and dimmed backgrounds. Mix no other hues into the art. For grey, dither blue and white.

**Era.** Draw in 16-bit SNES-style pixel art. Give each shape chunky two-pixel outlines and flat fills, shade with limited dithering, hold hard edges, and add no anti-aliasing, no smoothing, and no blur. Draw character sprites 32 to 64 pixels tall. Build backgrounds from layered flats in the manner of parallax scrolling. Take your reference from early-1990s console RPGs and platformers, upscaled clean so the pixels stay visible.

**Character sheet: Cosmo.** Draw Cosmo as a pixel cougar sprite with tan fur #F5E6C8, a white muzzle and chest patch, navy outlines, small rounded ears, and a long tail with a navy tip. He wears the BYU jersey in blue #002E5D with a large white block Y on the chest and white trim on the sleeves. Give him an athletic build, broad shoulders, big expressive eyes, a wide grin, white gloves, and white sneakers. He carries props by scene: a pencil the size of a sword, a notepad shield, a rolled scroll, a compass, a torch. His idle pose is a slight lean forward, weight on the front foot.

**Character sheet: Player 2.** Player 2 is the interviewer and the co-op partner. Draw a pixel human in a navy blazer over a white shirt, with skin tone dithered from tan and white, short dark pixel hair, a clipboard in hand, and a calm half-smile. Keep Player 2's face friendly in each scene. Match Cosmo's sprite scale.

**Character sheet: Dana Okafor.** Dana Okafor is the fictional CEO of Wasatch Wheels. Draw a pixel woman in a tan ski jacket with navy trim, a navy beanie, goggles pushed up on her forehead, and a coffee cup in one hand. She appears on the chairlift and nowhere else.

**Recurring UI chrome.** Draw dialogue boxes as a navy panel with a white two-pixel border, white pixel text, a blinking white triangle cursor in the bottom right corner, and the speaker name in a small tab on the top left edge. Stack HUD bars in threes labeled HP, MP, XP, white frames with tan fill on a navy backing, pinned to the top left of the frame. Build inventory grids from navy slots with white borders, items drawn as 16-pixel icons, and a white pointing-hand cursor. Draw skill-tree nodes as round pixel medallions joined by white pixel lines, and leave unlocked nodes solid tan and locked nodes dithered. Draw overworld maps as top-down pixel terrain, tan land, navy water, white roads, and nodes shaped as flags, villages, or castles. Lay out menus as a vertical list with a white pointing-hand cursor.

**Campus elements.** Draw the Tanner Building as the Dungeon: a tall pixel tower of navy glass panels and tan stone with a wide entry arch and two white pixel torches. Draw Y Mountain as a pixel mountain with a large white Y on its slope, and use it on the three covers and the two chairlift scenes only. Draw the Provo valley as rows of tan rooftops and navy pines.

**Negative prompt in Midjourney syntax.** Append the line below to each generation. --no photorealism, gradients, soft shading, modern flat vector look, 3D render, blur, anti-aliasing, text other than the labels named in the prompt, misspelled text, real company logos, red, green, orange, purple, extra characters
